Playing online with a friend

One player starts a match and shares the 6-character room code. The other player enters that same code to join. Both devices need an internet connection.

Reporting or blocking a player

In an online match, long-press any chat message to report or block that player. Reported messages are recorded for review. We do not tolerate abusive or objectionable content.

Resetting your data

Your profile, stats, level and achievements are stored on your device. To clear them, go to Settings → Reset Local Data, or delete the app to remove everything.
