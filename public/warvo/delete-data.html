Delete Your Warvo Data

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Warvo account and the data associated with it. Warvo is a receipt and warranty organizer. An account is optional — it is only used to sync your receipts across your devices.

Delete your account in the app (fastest)

If you are signed in to Warvo:

  1. Open Warvo and go to Settings.
  2. Under Cloud sync, tap your account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your Warvo account and all receipt data and images that were synced to the cloud. Receipts stored only on your device are not affected by account deletion — see below to remove those.

Remove receipts stored on your device

Receipts you have not synced live only on your device. To remove them, delete them individually inside the app, or uninstall Warvo — uninstalling removes all locally stored receipts and app data from that device.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us and we'll delete your account and any cloud data for you:

  • Email: contact@hilalalhinai.com
  • Subject: Delete my Warvo data
  • Please send it from the email address associated with your account so we can verify the request.

We will process verified deletion requests and confirm completion within 30 days.
