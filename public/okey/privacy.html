Privacy Policy — Okey: The Turkish Game

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Okey does not collect, store or share any personal information.

The name you enter is kept only on your own device and is shown to the other players in a room you join. It is not sent anywhere else and is not stored on any server.

Online play

Online games are carried by Unity's Relay service, which passes messages between players. No game data is retained after a game ends.

No ads or tracking

Okey contains no adverts, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking.
