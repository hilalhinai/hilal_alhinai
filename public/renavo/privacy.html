Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 August 2026 Last updated: 3 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Renavo ("Renavo", the "App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. It is provided by Hilal Al Hinai ("the Provider"). If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

Summary

Renavo helps you track your car's service history, fuel, expenses, documents and renewal reminders. By design, your data is stored locally on your device. You can start in guest mode with no account. We only process personal data beyond your device if you choose to sign in, or when you make a purchase. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show ads.

1. Information we process

a) Vehicle and record data you create (stored on your device). When you add a vehicle, service, fuel entry, expense, reminder or document, the App stores that information on your device — including dates, mileage, costs, workshop names, notes, and any invoices, registration or insurance documents and photos you attach. This data stays in local storage on your device unless you choose to sign in and enable backup or sync.

b) Account and sign-in (optional). Renavo does not require an account. You can use the App in guest mode. If you choose to sign in, you may do so with your email address, Google, or Apple. When you sign in we process an account identifier and the name and email associated with your chosen method, so we can secure your account and, where you enable it, back up or sync your records across your devices.

c) Purchases and subscriptions. Renavo Premium is processed by Apple through the App Store. We process purchase and subscription information (such as product identifiers, and purchase and expiration dates) to unlock and restore Premium features. We do not receive your full payment card details.

d) Reminders and notifications. Service and renewal reminders are scheduled and shown locally by your device. They are not sent from our servers.

e) Support. If you contact us (for example, by email), we receive the information you provide, including your email address and message contents, to respond to you.

We do not show advertising, we do not sell your personal information, and we do not collect your contacts, precise location, health data, or browsing history.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above to: operate core features (store, search and display your vehicles, records, documents and reminders); provide optional account sign-in and, where enabled, backup and sync; process and restore purchases and unlock Premium; deliver local reminders; respond to support requests; maintain security and prevent abuse; and comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases (EEA/UK – GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract (providing the App and features you request, including account sign-in and purchases); legitimate interests (security and preventing abuse); and consent (any optional feature you switch on, such as backup or sync). You may withdraw consent at any time by disabling the relevant feature.

4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers acting on our behalf or that you engage by using a feature: Apple (App Store purchases), and the sign-in provider you choose (Apple, Google, or an email sign-in provider). We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety and security. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Data retention

Records stored on your device remain until you delete them or uninstall the App. If you sign in and enable backup or sync, that data remains until you delete the record or your account. Purchase records are retained by Apple as needed for their services and legal requirements.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. California residents have rights to know, delete, and correct personal information and to not be discriminated against for exercising them; we do not sell or "share" personal information as defined by the CCPA.

You can exercise many of these rights directly in the App: delete individual records, delete your account, or uninstall to remove local data. For other requests, contact us at contact@hilalalhinai.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. EEA/UK users may also complain to their local data protection authority.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including on-device storage and access controls on any account data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. Children

Renavo is not directed to children and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new "Last updated" date, and, where required, provide additional notice.
