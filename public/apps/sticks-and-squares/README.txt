App icon
--------
  icon.png   — square, 1024 x 1024, no rounded corners needed

Screenshots
-----------
Drop three portrait PNGs here, named exactly:

  screen-1.png
  screen-2.png
  screen-3.png

Recommended size: 1290 x 2796 (iPhone 6.7"). App Store screenshots
work as-is — no cropping needed. Export at full resolution; small
images look blurry in the phone frames.

Referenced from src/lib/apps.ts under the 'sticks-and-squares' entry.
Current alt text assumes:

  screen-1: board mid-game
  screen-2: online multiplayer / chat
  screen-3: themes / profile / achievements

If your screens differ, say so and the alt text can be updated.

Note: the game's legal pages live separately at public/sticks-and-squares/
(privacy.html, support.html) — not in this folder.
