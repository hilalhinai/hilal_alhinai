Warvo — app page assets
=======================

App icon
--------
  icon.png   — square, 1024 x 1024, no rounded corners needed
               (the site rounds them). Same artwork as App Store Connect.

Screenshots
-----------
Drop three portrait PNGs here, named exactly:

  screen-1.png
  screen-2.png
  screen-3.png

Recommended size: 1290 x 2796 (iPhone 6.7"). App Store screenshots
work as-is — no cropping needed. Export at full resolution; small
images look blurry in the phone frames.

Referenced from src/lib/apps.ts under the 'warvo' entry. Current
alt text assumes:

  screen-1: warranty dashboard (active / expiring / expired)
  screen-2: scanning a receipt
  screen-3: instant search

If your screens differ, say so and the alt text can be updated.

Note: Warvo's legal pages live separately at public/warvo/
(privacy.html, terms.html) — not in this folder.
